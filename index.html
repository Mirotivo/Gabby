About Us

Passion for Food,
Dedication to Excellence

Top and Tasty Catering has been serving Sydney's North Ryde community and beyond for over 15 years. Our philosophy is simple: use the freshest locally sourced ingredients, apply expert culinary technique, and deliver with flawless service.

Whether you're planning a corporate lunch for 20, a wedding reception for 300, or a casual weekend BBQ, our team of professional chefs and event specialists will craft a bespoke experience tailored to your vision.

  • Fresh, locally sourced ingredients
  • Fully licensed and insured
  • Dietary & allergy-friendly options
  • Professional, uniformed staff
