About Us

Good Food,
Handled with Care

Top and Tasty Catering was founded in 2021 right here in Strathfield. We're a small, passionate team that genuinely loves what we do — preparing fresh, tasty food and making sure every event runs smoothly.

We specialise in corporate events, school functions, community gatherings, and private celebrations. No event is too big or too small — we'll work with your budget and make it work.

  • Fresh, quality ingredients
  • Flexible packages for any budget
  • Dietary & allergy-friendly options
  • Friendly, reliable team
